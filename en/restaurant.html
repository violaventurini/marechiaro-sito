Before you come

Frequently asked questions

How does lunch work?

From 12 noon we serve at the table facing the sea. Otherwise you can order takeaway and collect it at the counter.

In the evening, are you a restaurant or a pizzeria?

Both. You can have dinner à la carte — daily specials based on the catch, salads, seafood mains — or a thin Roman pizza, with the oven on until late.

What are the kitchen hours?

Breakfast at the bar from 9, lunch from 12 noon, aperitivo from 6pm, and in the evening restaurant and pizzeria.

Do you speak English?

Yes, and the menu is bilingual. You can also write to us on WhatsApp in English.

Do I need to book for dinner?

On weekdays usually not. At weekends, for groups and in August it's better to book — a message is enough.

Can I come for dinner without spending the day on the beach?

Of course. Restaurant, pizzeria and aperitivo are open to everyone, you don't need to have hired anything.
