Before you write

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?

For a group table a couple of days is enough. For a private evening allow two or three weeks, especially in August.

How is the booking confirmed?

We agree it when you confirm. The balance is settled at the end of the evening.

Can I bring a cake or decorations?

Yes, cakes are welcome, and we can agree on decorations — they just need to be easy to put up and take down.

Is there a minimum number of people?

For a reserved table we start at ten people. Below that we'll still seat you, just without exclusive use of the gazebo.

Are drinks included?

It depends on how we set up the evening — we agree it when we define the menu. You always get a written proposal first, with no surprises.

What kind of events do you host?

Birthdays, group dinners, hen and stag parties, graduation parties, company dinners and anniversaries. Always on the beach, facing the sea.
