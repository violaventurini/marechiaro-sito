Frequently asked questions

How do I get to Marechiaro from Rome?

Take the Roma–Lido train from Piramide to Lido Centro, then it's a five minute walk towards the sea. The ticket is a standard Rome public transport ticket. By car, take Via del Mare or Via Cristoforo Colombo to Lungomare Toscanelli.

Do I need a car?

No. The Roma–Lido train runs from Piramide, which is on Metro line B, and Lido Centro station is five minutes away on foot.

Where can I park?

Along Lungomare Toscanelli and in the side streets. There's also a cycle path right outside, with bike racks at the entrance.

Is it a good day trip from Rome?

It's the closest sea to the city and you can do it without a car. Many people pair it with the Roman ruins of Ostia Antica, ten minutes away.

What are your opening hours?

Every day from 9 until late. Lunch from 1pm, aperitivo from 6pm, restaurant and pizzeria in the evening.
