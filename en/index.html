Is the restaurant open for lunch and dinner?

Yes. At lunch, from 12 noon, there are the day’s seafood dishes, fried food, salads, sandwiches and pizza, at the table or to take away. In the evening there’s an à la carte dinner with a sea view, and a pizzeria. See the full Marechiaro menu.

Do I need to book a table?

It’s recommended, especially at weekends and for groups: just send us a WhatsApp message.

Do the beach huts have a shower?

Yes, the beach huts have a shower and changing room. There are also hot showers and toilets for everyone on the beach.

Can I come for dinner without spending the day at the beach?

Of course. The restaurant, pizzeria and aperitivo are open to everyone, even if you haven’t rented a sunbed or umbrella.

Can I organise a party or private event?

Yes: birthdays, graduation parties, hen parties, company dinners, group dinners and private evenings on the beach. We set the tables with a sea view and agree the menu with you. See parties and private events at Marechiaro.

Where is Marechiaro?

At Lungomare Paolo Toscanelli 27, Lido di Ostia, in the city of Rome: about one kilometre from Lido Centro station and twenty minutes from Fiumicino airport.

How do I get to Marechiaro from central Rome?

By car or taxi it takes about half an hour along Via Cristoforo Colombo or Via del Mare to Lungomare Toscanelli. By train, take metro line B to Piramide, then the Roma–Lido train from Porta San Paolo to Lido Centro; from the station it’s about a kilometre’s walk to the sea.

How far is Marechiaro from Fiumicino airport?

About 20 minutes by taxi or car along the coast: the most convenient beach if you’re arriving or leaving by plane.

Is there luggage storage?

Yes. If you’re arriving in Rome or flying out of Fiumicino, you can leave your suitcases in our luggage storage and enjoy a day at the beach, with a shower and beach hut to change before your flight. The airport is about 20 minutes away by taxi. For information, message us on WhatsApp.
