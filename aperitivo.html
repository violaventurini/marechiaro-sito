Before you come

Frequently asked questions

What time does aperitivo start?

From 6pm, every day. Sunset on the Roman coast falls shortly after, and it's the best moment of the day.

Do I need to have spent the day on the beach?

No. Aperitivo, dinner and after-dinner are open to everyone. You just walk in.

Are sunbeds and umbrellas bookable?

For two people, no. For a group or a front-row table it's worth sending a message in the afternoon.

What is there to drink and eat?

Classic cocktails, beer, wine by the glass and soft drinks. To eat, fried bites and sharing boards. If you stay for dinner, the restaurant menu is the same.

How late can I stay?

The kitchen works until late and the bar stays open after. After dinner is the quietest part: low music and one last drink on the sand.

Can I book the place for a private evening?

Yes, for birthdays and parties. Tell us what you have in mind and we’ll send a tailored proposal.
